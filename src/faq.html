Frequently Asked Questions

What does Cosimo do?

Cosimo is fund operations software from Medici & Company. It prepares draft investor reports, portfolio updates, and replies from the records your fund already keeps. We configure each workflow around your sources, reporting format, and review process.

Which funds is it for?

Our examples cover real estate, venture capital, private equity, private credit, and hedge funds. Start with a recurring report or document your team still prepares by hand. We work through the requirements with you before choosing a first workflow to test.

Do we have to change our reporting format?

Your existing report is the starting point. We agree which figures, commentary, headings, and checks the draft needs, then configure and test the workflow with your team.

Does Cosimo send reports to investors?

The workflows shown here prepare drafts for your team to review. Your team checks the figures, resolves open questions, approves the final version, and sends it through your usual process.

Are the website examples live product runs?

No. They use fictional records and prewritten outputs to explain the proposed workflow. They show the sources, draft documents, and review questions. We configure and test the workflow for your fund before agreeing what it can handle.

How will you work with our systems and data requirements?

Before starting, we agree how records will be supplied, who needs access, and your requirements for handling fund data. Tell us which systems you use and what your team needs to verify so we can review the proposed setup together.

How do we start?

Email info@medici.ai with the report or process that takes too much of your team’s time. A few sentences are enough: what you prepare, where the information comes from, and which part is slowest. We’ll identify a first task to test with your team and agree the scope and commercial terms before starting.
