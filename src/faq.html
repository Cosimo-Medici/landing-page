Frequently Asked Questions

What does Cosimo do?

Cosimo is an AI agent for financial operations from Medici & Company. It gives teams more capacity by taking on recurring reports, reconciliations and follow-ups using their own records and procedures. We configure the sources, calculations, output and review requirements with your team, then retain the agreed method for subsequent runs.

Who is Cosimo for?

Cosimo is for banks, funds, insurance teams, and administration, accounting and advisory firms with recurring manual work. Banks can start with loan activity reports, borrower review packages, or account-file checks. Fund examples cover real estate, venture capital, private equity, private credit, and hedge funds. Insurance teams can explore renewal preparation, submission checks, and claims-file summaries. Administration, accounting, and advisory firms can start with client reporting and document collection. We assess fit against the specific work, records, and review requirements.

What work can we start with?

Choose a recurring task with identifiable records and a clear output: a daily loan activity report, investor update, renewal checklist, or client reporting package. We agree the inputs, output, and acceptance criteria with your team, then configure and test the first process.

Does Cosimo replace our administrator or accountant?

Cosimo works alongside your operations team, administrator, accountant, and other advisers. It takes on preparation and coordination: gathering records, checking figures, and assembling work for review. It is not your administrator of record. Accounting, valuation, professional sign-off, and business decisions remain with the people responsible for them.

Do we have to change our reporting format?

Your existing report is the starting point. We agree the figures, calculations, commentary, headings, and checks it needs, then configure and test the process with your team. The agreed method and format are saved for the next reporting period.

What happens on the next reporting cycle?

The agreed sources, calculations, checks and output format are saved as a process. Use it with the next period’s records. If the portfolio, terms, source format or reporting requirements change, we update the process with your team. Your team continues to resolve exceptions and provide the required approvals.

How does Cosimo fit alongside our existing AI tools?

Start with the recurring job you want done. Cosimo is configured to bring the records through the agreed checks and calculations into a useful output, with the method saved for the next run. Compare that process with how your team performs the same job today, including the effort needed to correct, review and finish the work.

What happens when information is missing or figures disagree?

Cosimo flags missing information and discrepancies for review, with references back to the records used. Your team can inspect the figures, resolve the question, and approve any change. The examples on this site show that process: a possible duplicate in a bank activity file, a conflicting insurance submission, and differences between portfolio-company accounts and commentary.

Who controls what Cosimo sends or does?

We agree the permitted actions and review requirements with your team during setup. Cosimo can prepare work and perform supported actions through connected accounts. Sending to other people and editing or deleting existing material require individual approval. Some actions confined to your own account can be allowed on an ongoing basis. The website examples use prepared outputs and do not send messages or move money.

What do the website examples show?

The interactive examples use fictional records and prepared outputs to show the work: inputs, calculations, reports and questions for review. They let you inspect the output and supporting records. For your evaluation, we agree the acceptance criteria, run the configured process using agreed source records and assess both the output and the work left for your team.

How will Cosimo work with our systems?

Cosimo works with documents, spreadsheets, and email, with connections to Google Drive, SharePoint, Gmail, Outlook, and Google Calendar. We agree which sources and permissions your process needs and verify the setup with your team. An evaluation can start with agreed file exports; any connection to a core banking, policy, claims, or accounting system needs a separate scope review.

How do we assess data handling and security?

We review access, data handling, retention, and your security requirements before agreeing an evaluation. Client data is restricted to that client’s work and is not used to train shared models. We do not currently have a SOC 2 report; a SOC 2 examination is on our roadmap. Tell us which evidence and approvals your vendor review requires so we can establish whether the proposed setup meets them.

How do we evaluate Cosimo, and what does it cost?

Start with one recurring job. We agree the scope, data requirements and NDA before you share source files, then confirm feasibility in writing and configure the process with your team. Evaluate the output against its sources and count the total staff effort across preparation, correction, review and follow-up. Record elapsed turnaround separately. Model usage is charged at cost, with no markup; platform scope and commercial terms are agreed before starting. Timing depends on the records, work and review requirements.

How do we start?

Email info@medici.ai with the process that takes too much of your team’s time. A few sentences are enough: what you prepare, where the information comes from, and which part is slowest. We’ll assess fit, identify a first task to test with your team, and agree the scope before starting.
