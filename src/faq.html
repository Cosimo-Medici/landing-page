Frequently Asked Questions

What does Cosimo do?

Cosimo is an AI agent for financial operations from Medici & Company. It prepares recurring reports, checks, follow-ups, and review packs from the records your firm already keeps. We configure each process around your sources, calculations, output format, and approval requirements, then retain that method for subsequent runs.

Who is Cosimo for?

Cosimo is for banking, fund, insurance, and financial-service teams with recurring manual work. Banks can start with loan activity reports, borrower review packs, or account-file checks. Fund examples cover real estate, venture capital, private equity, private credit, and hedge funds. Insurance teams can explore renewal preparation, submission checks, and claims-file summaries. Administration, accounting, and advisory firms can start with client reporting and document collection. We assess fit against the specific work, records, and review requirements.

What work can we start with?

Start with a recurring task whose records and expected output you can identify: a daily loan activity report, borrower review pack, account-file checklist, investor report, portfolio update, or client reporting package. We agree the inputs, output, and acceptance criteria with your team, then configure and test the first process.

Does Cosimo replace our administrator or accountant?

Today, Cosimo handles internal fund operations alongside your administrator, accountant, and other advisers. It reduces the preparation, coordination, and review work your team still carries between those providers. It is not your administrator of record. Accounting, valuation, professional sign-off, and investment decisions remain with the people responsible for them.

Do we have to change our reporting format?

Your existing report is the starting point. We agree the figures, calculations, commentary, headings, and checks it needs, then configure and test the process with your team. The agreed method and format are saved for the next reporting period.

What happens when information is missing or figures disagree?

Extracted figures retain their source location so the work can be checked. A second model re-reads the source to verify extracted values, while calculation scripts perform the arithmetic and reconciliation checks. Discrepancies and missing information become findings for review. In our tested synthetic-fund quarterly report run, Cosimo raised five exceptions, including a DSCR breach, a missing covenant, an unpaid subscription, and two data gaps. Your team decides how those findings are resolved.

Who controls what Cosimo sends or does?

Your team sets the approval requirements for each process and user. Cosimo can prepare and send communications through connected accounts; an email, filing, or portal action waits for approval when the process is configured that way. Permissions distinguish actions that can proceed, require approval, or are denied. The website’s reporting examples show drafts for review, and the investor notices shown here do not execute payments.

Is Cosimo in use, and how has it been tested?

Yes. Cosimo is used daily by live clients, and our team validates fund processes against their source records. On synthetic demonstration funds, we have tested nine of ten real estate processes figure by figure, including a quarterly report run for five properties and eight investors whose figures matched an 88-row source sheet to the cent. The calculation library includes 83 tested scripts with 1,822 passing tests. The interactive website examples illustrate that work; they are not recordings of those runs.

How will Cosimo work with our systems?

Cosimo works with documents, spreadsheets, and email, and supports connections to Google Drive, SharePoint, Gmail, Outlook, and Google Calendar. We agree which sources and permissions your first process needs and verify the setup with your team. Connecting a system does not mean every task within it is ready without configuration.

How do we assess data handling and security?

Before starting, we review access, data handling, retention, and any security requirements your firm needs to satisfy. Client data is restricted to that client’s work and is not used to train shared models. SOC 2 certification is on our roadmap; we do not currently claim to be certified. If certification is a requirement for your firm, raise it at the outset.

How do we evaluate Cosimo, and what does it cost?

Start by naming one recurring task and sharing one period of source files under NDA with read-only access. We confirm feasibility in writing, run the process, and walk through the output and its sources with you. Model usage is charged at cost, with no markup; platform scope and commercial terms are agreed in conversation. Timing depends on the records, task, and review requirements.

How do we start?

Email info@medici.ai with the process that takes too much of your team’s time. A few sentences are enough: what you prepare, where the information comes from, and which part is slowest. We’ll assess fit, identify a first task to test with your team, and agree the scope before starting.
