Setup questions ↗

  1. Choose the work.

    Pick one recurring report or request. Show us the format you use and explain where preparation gets stuck.

  2. Configure and test it.

    Agree which records and definitions to use. Check a trial draft against the sources and identify what still needs your team’s judgment.

  3. Review each draft.

    Your team checks the figures, resolves open questions, and approves what goes out. When the next report is due, start with the new records.
