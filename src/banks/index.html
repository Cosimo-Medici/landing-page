From the core export
to the morning review.

Start with the daily loan activity report. We configure the steps around your export, loan terms and review procedures, then save that method for the next day’s records.

  1. 01 / Inputs

    Use the files you already have.

    Start with a core-system export, the relevant loan schedule, and supporting documents. Agree which fields and records the report needs.

  2. 02 / Preparation

    Separate activity. Check the details.

    Group payments and advances, reconcile the movement to the closing balance, and identify discrepancies or missing information.

  3. 03 / Review

    Put the exceptions in front of an officer.

    Prepare the activity summary, supporting records, and questions still outstanding. Your team reviews the findings and decides what happens next.
