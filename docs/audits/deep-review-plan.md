# Deep review plan — 7 October 2026

The earlier website audit was a first pass, not adequate support for a conclusive quality verdict. This pass evaluates the actual visitor experience and the sales argument, not just source correctness.

## Review lanes

1. RE fund buyer: current tasks, existing administrator/Excel alternatives, property and investor evidence.
2. VC fund buyer: portfolio monitoring, stale data, cash/runway, partner/CFO relevance.
3. PE fund buyer: inconsistent company submissions, EBITDA definitions, performance explanations, LP reporting.
4. Private-credit buyer: servicing information, covenant definitions, exceptions, and committee/LP audiences.
5. Hedge-fund buyer: administrator records, share classes, reconciliation, allocator responses.
6. Visitor journey: cold-email landing, homepage-to-sector, sample inspection, concern resolution, contact.
7. Visual design: actual desktop/mobile screenshots, hierarchy, pacing, legibility, memorable brand character.
8. Interaction resilience: native browser controls, reader/source relationships, keyboard/mobile states, layout while headlines rotate.
9. Adversarial integration review after changes: disagreements resolved with evidence; check regressions and new overclaims.

Parallelism is limited to three child agents at once. Lanes run in waves; the number of agents is not a substitute for distinct questions or evidence.

## Evidence required

- Each sector reviewer reads every page section and worked document, checks the financial example and narrative consistency, uses relevant primary sources, and inspects captured desktop/mobile screenshots.
- State whether a finding is reproduced in a browser, demonstrated in source/data, grounded in research, or a design judgment. Simulated buyer reactions are expert inferences, not real-user tests.
- Each serious finding needs an owner, concrete change or explicit disposition, and a verification method.
- Preserve the approved branding and independent randomized two-track headline behavior. Preserve document depth; do not hide useful information merely to make a page short.
- Do not fabricate customer proof, product behavior, integrations, timing, savings, or booking destinations. The app environment is taken as supplied; the website's illustrative demos remain labeled.
- Assess whether the visitor can identify who it is for, what work it does, the practical benefit, how the example supports it, and the next action. Do not pretend a one-second understanding of every financial detail is measurable without prospects.

## Completion criteria

- Findings from all nine lanes considered; high-impact fixable problems resolved and tested.
- Sector examples internally coherent across hero documents, inputs, calculations, commentary, and internal/investor audiences.
- The visitor journey has a clear starting point, a concise commercial case, explorable evidence, practical setup expectations, and an accurate contact action.
- Browser verification of changed interactions/layouts and a skeptical second pass on the integrated result.
- Remaining dependencies separated from defects that can be fixed in this repository. No merge or deployment.
